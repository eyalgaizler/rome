Your base — one hotel, right in the centre

Hotel dei Borgognoni (recommended) — a calm 4-star on a quiet courtyard street in the Tridente, a short walk from both the Spanish Steps and the Trevi Fountain. Clean, safe, central and lively enough that the walk back at night is busy and well-lit — never isolated.

No car needed all trip: everything central is walkable, the metro is cheap and easy, and Florence is a fast train from Roma Termini.

🚶 On foot
Before you go — good to know

Practical notes

Your hotel

Hotel dei Borgognoni (recommended) — clean 4-star in the Tridente, between the Spanish Steps and Trevi. Central, safe and never isolated; the walk back at night is busy and well-lit. Confirm price & availability when you book.

Getting around — no car

The centre is walkable; the metro & buses are cheap (single ticket €1.50, or a 48/72-hr pass). From the airport: Leonardo Express train to Termini (~€14) or a fixed-price taxi (~€50 to the centre). Florence is a fast train from Termini.

Staying safe at night

Rome's centre, Trastevere and Campo de' Fiori are busy and lively late — stick to well-lit main streets, keep bags zipped and to the front (pickpockets work crowds & metro), and use official white taxis or an app. Emergency: 112.

Food — seafood-free

Every restaurant here is picked for Roman classics — carbonara, cacio e pepe, amatriciana, pizza, saltimbocca — so it's easy to eat brilliantly with no seafood. Reserve the popular trattorias a few days ahead.

Book ahead

Colosseum (timed ticket), Vatican Museums (skip-the-line), Florence train + Uffizi/Accademia, and the top trattorias. Booking online saves hours of queuing.

Money & tipping

Italy uses the euro; cards work almost everywhere. Tipping is modest — round up or leave a euro or two; check the bill for "coperto" (cover charge) and "servizio". Carry a little cash for markets and gelato.

Weather & packing

Late October in Rome is mild — days ~19–22°C, cooler evenings ~11–13°C, the odd shower. Bring layers, a light jacket, an umbrella and comfortable shoes for the cobbles.
