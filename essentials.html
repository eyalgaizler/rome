🧳 Packing list

Late October — mild days, cool evenings

  • Comfortable walking shoes (lots of cobbles)
  • Layers + a light jacket for evenings
  • Compact umbrella / light rain layer
  • One dressier outfit for nights out
  • Sunglasses & a scarf
  • Cross-body bag that zips (pickpocket-safe)
  • Reusable water bottle (free public fountains!)
  • Power bank + cables
  • EU adapter (Italy: type C / F / L)
  • Some cash in euro for markets & gelato
  • Shoulders covered for churches (Vatican dress code)

📄 Documents

Check a week before the flight

  • Passports valid 6+ months past return date
  • Travel & health insurance (policy number saved)
  • Flight confirmation + return details
  • Hotel: Monti House, Monti, Rome
  • Colosseum & Vatican Museums tickets (timed)
  • Rome ⇄ Florence train tickets + Uffizi/Accademia
  • Digital copies of everything on your phones
  • Emergency & insurance contact numbers

💶 Money & tipping

Currency: euro (€)

  • Currency Euro €
  • Cards Accepted almost everywhere
  • Cash Handy for markets & gelato
  • "Coperto" Small cover charge is normal
  • Tipping Modest — round up / €1–2
  • ATMs Use bank ATMs; decline "conversion"

🗣️ Useful phrases

Italian

  • Hello / Good morning Ciao / Buongiorno
  • Good evening Buonasera
  • Thank you Grazie
  • Please Per favore
  • Yes / No Sì / No
  • Excuse me Scusi
  • How much is it? Quanto costa?
  • Water Acqua
  • The bill, please Il conto, per favore
  • Cheers! Salute!
